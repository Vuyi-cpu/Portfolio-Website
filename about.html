Background

Who I Am

I'm Vuyisa Msipa, a web developer and digital designer based in Johannesburg, South Africa, currently studying towards a degree in Digital Arts at Wits University (DIGA4014A/3008A). I'm passionate about the intersection of technology and design — building things that are both technically sound and visually meaningful.

My journey into development started through curiosity about how things work on the web. That curiosity grew into a genuine love for crafting interactive experiences, writing clean code, and solving creative problems through a structured, thoughtful process.

I bring a multidisciplinary perspective to every project — combining front-end development, UI thinking, and an understanding of game mechanics to create experiences that engage users and communicate clearly.
